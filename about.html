🎓 About SkillForge

SkillForge is a modern online learning platform focused on practical, job-ready skills. We believe education should be accessible, project-driven and continuously updated with industry needs.

Our instructors are working professionals from leading tech, design and business companies. Every course includes hands-on projects, quizzes and a certificate of completion.

Founded to close the skills gap, we now serve learners in 90+ countries.
